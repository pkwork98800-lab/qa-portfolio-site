About Me

QA engineer focused on AI agent reliability

Building Trust in AI Systems

I specialize in QA infrastructure for AI agents — the evaluation harnesses, regression gates, and observability pipelines that make non-deterministic systems auditable and trustworthy.

My flagship project, agent-qa-lab, demonstrates a complete evaluation pipeline: a mini-CRM with a tool-calling agent, 60 scenarios spanning deterministic assertions, LLM-as-judge layers, ground-truth database verification, red-team probes, and judge calibration — all running offline at $0 real cost.

Currently building: eval-regression-gate (statistical merge gate for LLM evals) and web-qa-fundamentals (full-stack E2E with accessibility budget and visual regression).
